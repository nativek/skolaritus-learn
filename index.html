How We Work

Session Formats

Choose the format that suits your learning style and budget — or combine them across different subjects.

01

One-on-One Sessions

Fully personalised — the tutor focuses exclusively on your pace, gaps, and exam needs. Ideal for targeted intervention, exam preparation, or acceleration. Sessions run 60–90 minutes via Microsoft Teams.

02

Group Sessions

2–6 students at the same grade and subject level. The social dynamic encourages peer discussion and shared problem-solving. More affordable per student. Great for revision weeks.

03

Guided Learning Packages

Structured monthly programmes spanning full terms or exam periods. Progress milestones, regular parent feedback, and consistent weekly coverage. Sold as monthly subscriptions.

04

Exam Intensive Blocks

Holiday crash courses (4–5 days) for upcoming Matric, BGCSE, or SGCSE exams. Focuses on past-paper technique, examiner patterns, and high-yield topics. Available during June/October exam seasons.
